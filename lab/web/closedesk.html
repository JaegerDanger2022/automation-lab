    How it works

    1. Receive. An n8n webhook takes the invoice, opens a run in the log and checks the visitor's daily limit and the monthly budget.
    2. Read. PDFs with a text layer go to Claude as text. Scans and photos go through vision. Claude answers through a fixed tool schema with a confidence score per field.
    3. Check. Python rules, no model involved: bank account on file, duplicates (even when the number is written differently), arithmetic, purchase order, price against the PO, quantity against delivery notes, and low confidence.
    4. Decide and record. Fraud signals are held and need a written reason to release. Other problems go to an approver. Clean invoices post double-entry lines to the ledger, all in one transaction with an audit trail.
